## What this is

This directory gathers personal sites run by real people, scattered across the web, so that others can find them, read them and subscribe to them. The maintainer looks at every site before listing it, and each entry says whose site it is and what they write or make.

<!-- STATS:START -->
- **48** independent sites from **9** regions, written in 5 languages.
- **19** of them publish an RSS / Atom feed — import the whole list at once with [feeds.opml](https://realchendahuang.github.io/one-person-one-site/feeds.opml).
- Content types: blog (32), digital-garden (6), notes (10), portfolio (19), newsletter (1).
- Common topics: developer (35), ai (13), maker (11), indie-hacker (11), open-source (5), writer (5), designer (4), security (2).
<!-- STATS:END -->

Behind every entry is a specific person: a manufacturing engineer documenting his AI experiments, an interior designer writing about building products while running a studio, a university student growing his studies into a digital garden, a senior engineer sorting out lessons on RAG and backend systems. They write about different things. What they share is that their work lives on a site of their own, not only on a platform.

## How this differs from other directories

Plenty of projects already collect personal blogs, each with its own focus:

- For a list of Chinese blogs that aims to be complete, see [chinese-independent-blogs](https://github.com/timqian/chinese-independent-blogs).
- For blog webrings and long-term pledges, see [Travellings](https://www.travellings.cn/) and [Foreverblog](https://www.foreverblog.cn/).

One Person, One Site works this way:

- **Small and curated.** Every site is checked by hand. Sites that do not fit are not listed, and listed sites that turn into marketing pages are re-evaluated.
- **About people, not just links.** Every entry has a factual one-line description and tags, so you can browse by role and topic.
- **More than blogs.** Personal portfolio sites of designers, developers and photographers are welcome too.
- **Easy to subscribe and reuse.** All feeds are bundled into one OPML file, and all data is open as JSON.

## What we believe

**A personal website is one of the few places on the internet that is still truly yours.**

A platform account can be throttled or banned, and the rules can change at any time. A domain and site of your own cannot: you decide how it looks, how it reads and what it holds, and it can quietly stay online for a decade.

That is why we keep this directory: so that people who write and make things carefully are easier to find.

> One Person, One Site does not ask everyone to become a full-time blogger. We want to find people who already write and create on their own sites over the long term, and introduce them to more readers.

## What we list

### ✅ Welcome

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Independent Blog** | Long-term writing on technology, thinking, books or life, with a real author behind it. |
| **Digital Garden** | Unfinished but steadily growing notes and ideas, linked together. |
| **Portfolio** | A personal site where a designer, developer, photographer or artist shows their own work. |
| **Indie Maker** | Building in public: records of personal projects and explorations. |
| **Knowledge Base** | Systematically organized public notes on a particular field. |

### ❌ Not listed

- Corporate websites, marketing pages, SaaS landing pages;
- Freelance service pages and studio pricing pages;
- Platform products themselves, such as rankings, link directories, tool sites or API documentation sites;
- Single accounts on social platforms (WeChat, Xiaohongshu, Jike, Zhihu, X, …);
- Scraped or mirrored content, SEO farms;
- AI-generated content farms with no personal perspective;
- Sites with fraud, malicious redirects, infringement or illegal content.

### What we actually look at

1. **A real person is behind it.** You can tell who runs it and what they keep writing or making, not an anonymous shell.
2. **The content is their own.** Original articles, notes or work, not aggregation and reposts.
3. **There is already content.** At least a few original posts or pieces of work, not a freshly set-up empty site.
4. **It is for expression, not sales.** A portfolio presents its author, not a product or service for sale.
5. **It works.** Reachable, readable, without popup bombardment or malicious redirects.

A custom domain is not required. Hosted addresses such as `github.io`, `pages.dev` and `vercel.app` are fine.

> Put simply: **if you treat it as the place where you write or create for years, it belongs here.**

### On content boundaries

Being listed is not an endorsement. Opinions on those sites belong to their authors; we do not review opinions, only whether a site meets the criteria above. If a listed site later turns into a marketing page, changes hands, or hosts fraudulent content, please [report it](https://github.com/realchendahuang/one-person-one-site/issues/new?template=data-fix.yml) and we will re-evaluate.

## How to use this directory

**Browse it.** Open the [live directory](https://realchendahuang.github.io/one-person-one-site/) to filter by tag, search site names, authors and descriptions, or press "随机打开一个站点" (open a random site) to land somewhere you have never been. The [full directory page](https://realchendahuang.github.io/one-person-one-site/directory/) lists everything by tag and by name.

**Subscribe in bulk.** Download [feeds.opml](https://realchendahuang.github.io/one-person-one-site/feeds.opml) and import it into any RSS reader to follow every listed site that publishes a feed. It is more reliable than bookmarks: you see new posts as soon as they are published.

**Reuse the data.** All entries live in [data/sites.json](./data/sites.json), also served at [sites.json](https://realchendahuang.github.io/one-person-one-site/data/sites.json), under the MIT license. Build visualizations, a recommender, or feed it into other tools.

## Submit your site

New sites are welcome, especially ones that few people have found yet. Please check [what we list](#what-we-list) first.

**Option 1: Issue form (recommended)**
Use the [**site submission form**](https://github.com/realchendahuang/one-person-one-site/issues/new?template=submit-site.yml) with your site name, URL, owner name and a one-line description. The maintainer validates the format and checks that the site is reachable, usually within **a day or two**, and replies in your issue.

**Option 2: Pull request**
1. Fork and clone the repository;
2. Append your entry to `data/sites.json`:
   ```json
   {
     "name": "Your Site Name",
     "url": "https://your-domain.com",
     "owner": "Your Name",
     "description": "A backend engineer's blog with practical notes on distributed systems and databases.",
     "languages": ["en"],
     "region": "Global",
     "tags": ["blog", "developer", "notes"],
     "feed": "https://your-domain.com/rss.xml"
   }
   ```
3. Run `python3 scripts/validate.py && python3 scripts/generate_directory.py`;
4. Open a pull request.

> `feed` is optional. If your site publishes RSS / Atom, include it so your site is part of the subscription bundle.

### Fields

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `name` | ✅ | Site name, up to 40 characters |
| `url` | ✅ | Home page, not a single article |
| `owner` | ✅ | The author's usual name or handle |
| `description` | ✅ | 15–240 characters. Describe in the third person whose site it is and what it covers; no "worth listing" pitches |
| `languages` | ✅ | Main languages, e.g. `en`, `zh-CN`, `zh-TW`, `ja` |
| `region` | ✅ | e.g. `Global`, `China`, `Taiwan`, `Japan`, `US` |
| `tags` | ✅ | 1–8 lowercase tags, hyphenated when multi-word (`indie-hacker`) |
| `feed` | ⬜ | RSS / Atom / JSON Feed URL; use an array for several feeds, up to 5 |

### Recommended tags

| Category | Tags |
|---|---|
| **Content type** | `blog` · `digital-garden` · `notes` · `portfolio` · `newsletter` |
| **Author role** | `developer` · `designer` · `writer` · `maker` · `indie-hacker` · `photographer` · `researcher` |
| **Topic** | `ai` · `open-source` · `security` · `devops` · `game` · `tech` · `life` |

Please pick from this table. Other tags are not rejected, but `validate.py` will flag them and the maintainer may swap them for a close recommended tag.

## Maintaining the list

- **Found a dead site?** The maintainer periodically checks listed sites with a health-check script; you can also [report it](https://github.com/realchendahuang/one-person-one-site/issues/new?template=data-fix.yml) directly.
- **Need a correction?** Descriptions, tags and feed URLs can all be updated via issue or pull request.
- **Want to discuss what belongs here?** Open an issue and explain your reasoning.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md) for the detailed workflow.
